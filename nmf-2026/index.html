McDonald's of Central Texas at NightMarket.fun 2026.

Saturday, November 14, 2026 • Lakeline Park, Cedar Park, TX

On the main stage

Jimmy Ferguson represented McDonald's of Central Texas on the NightMarket.fun main stage, speaking alongside The Lunar Foundation's Andrew Lee, GAACC's Mark Duval, and Cedar Park Mayor Jim Penniman-Morin. Grimace joined him on stage. Cedar Park Councilwoman Shweta Padmanabha stopped for a photo with Jimmy.
